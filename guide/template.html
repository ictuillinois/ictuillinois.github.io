🚀 Getting Started

Signing in, finding your way around, and getting help

Signing in

{{APP}} runs in any modern browser — nothing to install. Go to:

Address: {{URL}}
  1. Enter the email and password your administrator gave you, then click Sign in.
  2. The first time, you are asked to choose a new password before you can continue.
  3. Leave "Keep me signed in on this device" ticked on a computer only you use. Untick it on a shared one.
  4. If the Terms of Service have changed since you last signed in, you are asked to accept them once.
Forgot your password? Ask your org admin to reset it, or use Contact Us on the sign-in page.
One person, two roles: someone who is both a lab manager and a lab user has two accounts on the same email. They sign in once; each account keeps its own permissions.

Section 02

👥 Lab Management

The people in your lab, account requests, and data export

Tabs: Lab Users Import lab users Lab Managers Organization Admins Approval Requests Export Data Lab Manager Guide

Lab users & lab managers

Lab Management → Lab Users / Lab Managers
  • Add — enter name and email. A temporary password is made and emailed; the person must choose a new one at their first sign-in.
  • Edit — details, assigned projects, and which dashboard icons the person gets.
  • Deactivate — the person can no longer sign in; their records are kept. Delete removes the account.
  • Adding someone whose email already has an account (for example a lab user who becomes a lab manager) links the new account to the same sign-in.
Organization Admins are shown read-only to lab managers. Only an org admin (or the super admin) can change an org admin's account.

Import lab users from Excel

Lab Management → Import lab users
  • Download the template, fill in first name, last name, email and director / advisor / supervisor / manager, and upload it. Up to 50 lab users per file — split a longer list into several files.
  • Review the list: edit any cell, remove or add a row, and tick the safety steps each person must do (Step 1 / 2 / 3) and Yearly re-safety. An unticked step is not required. A row whose email already has an account is shown in red and blocks the import until you remove or change it. Nothing is sent at this stage; Save and finish later keeps the list (for you and the other lab managers) to continue from the history.
  • Confirm — accounts are made only now — then choose to send the welcome email (sign-in email + temporary password) now or on a date and time you pick (Chicago time).
  • The safety steps an imported lab user completes count as approved straight away. People added one at a time still need your approval.
  • The history lists every import: who got the email, who has signed in and chosen their own password, and their safety progress. Scheduled emails can be sent now or moved; Resend email makes a new temporary password for someone who has not signed in.

Approval requests

Lab Management → Approval Requests

Requests to delete an account appear here. Before approving, choose who takes over that person's records, so nothing they own is lost. A count of pending requests shows at the top of Lab Management.

Export data

Lab Management → Export Data

Downloads one ZIP with a folder per person (lab users and lab managers separately). Each folder holds an Excel workbook — profile, every training record, bookings in the date range you choose, projects and materials — and the files they uploaded, sorted by source. Project folders hold a workbook per project and one folder per material. Anything that could not be included is listed on screen with the reason.

Section 03

🎓 Training Records

Everything a lab user completes and keeps current

Training Records opens on a grid of lab user cards. Click a card to open that person's records, then use the tabs inside the panel. The mark on each tab shows ✓ done, ⏳ waiting, or — not started. Switch This user / All users to see everyone's records at once — the audit view.

Privacy: a lab user's training records are visible only to them and to the lab managers and admins of their organization. A lab user can never approve their own records.

🦺 Safety

New lab users complete three steps: 1 watch the ICT safety training video; 2 read the Laboratory Safety Guide and complete the DRS online training; 3 watch both safety videos and pass the acknowledgement test. You can set which steps a person must do.

New lab users complete the safety steps in order. You can set which steps a person must do.

  • Approve each step when it is submitted. After an approval, a bar names the next step.
  • Some modules stay locked for a lab user until their safety training is approved.
  • Yearly re-safety: every Sep 1 each lab user with Yearly re-safety ticked (all lab users, unless you untick it in their Edit box) must pass Steps 1, 2 and 3 again, and ICT-Lab locks for them until they do. To excuse someone after Sep 1, untick their steps in Lab Management → Lab Users → Edit.

📄 Lab User Documents

Certificates the lab user uploads (and copies of their signed forms). Each shows its status; click Approve on a pending one.

🚗 Vehicle

Three sub-tabs: Training records Use agreement Forms.

  • Forms (lab user) — they tick the vehicles they need, read the Golf Cart Manual page by page and confirm, then download, sign and upload the two forms. A copy goes to their Documents.
  • Use agreement — the signed forms, with Approve / Deny.
  • Training records — vehicle training dates, and the vehicles each person asked for. Confirm unlocks only once their forms are approved and the manual read.
  • ICT vehicle list (sidebar, lab managers and admins) — the vehicles lab users can request. "Remove" hides a vehicle from new requests; past requests stay.

Vehicle training records for people who drive lab vehicles: date, trainer and confirmation.

Vehicle and building-alarm training dates cannot be edited once confirmed.

🔧 Equipment & retraining

Training per instrument, and the equipment exams passed. Equipment that needs training cannot be booked until it is approved. Equipment a lab user has not used for 3 months needs retraining: it is greyed out in Booking until the retraining is approved.

🗄️ Lab User Locker

  • Lab users see only their own locker. Without one, they press Request a locker (with an optional note).
  • You get a notification. The Locker requests box above the locker grid lists who is waiting; pick a free locker and Assign, or Decline. The request also shows on that person's Locker tab.
  • Mark lockers Unavailable in the grid; the All Lockers table lists every assignment.

🔔 Building Alarm, 📝 Exam & 🧯 SDS

  • Building Alarm — record alarm training, required for after-hours access.
  • Exam — equipment exam results: score, pass/fail and date.
  • SDS — the lab's safety data sheets. Lab managers upload PDFs, Word files, video or links; lab users can read them.
Section 04

🔧 Equipment & Maintenance

The equipment list, its records, and what lab users may see

Tabs: List of Equipment Calibration Maintenance Records Settings

Adding or editing equipment

  1. Click + Add equipment (or Import Excel to load a whole list).
  2. Enter name, nickname, category, location, manufacturer, serial number, date received and condition. Setting the condition to Out of Service marks it unusable.
  3. Tick Responsible for Maintenance — each lab manager is listed once — and whether they get maintenance reminders.
  4. Choose Lab user access (required):
    • Calibration & maintenance → Lab only: hidden from lab users, and from the SOP and booking lists for everyone unless "Show lab-only equipment" is turned on.
    • Or the areas lab users may use: Equipment SOP, Booking calendar, Request training, Exam. Unticking Request training means no training record is needed to book it.
  5. Save. An item with no choice yet shows Waiting for decision.

Maintenance Records shows usage hours from bookings and the maintenance history. Calibration holds calibration dates and records. Settings holds the equipment categories.

Section 05

📚 Equipment SOP

SOPs, videos, standards and exams for each instrument

  • Pick an instrument, then add or update its SOP, training videos, standards and exam questions.
  • Lab users see videos and SOPs once their training for that instrument is approved, or with temporary access you grant.
  • SOP Notes — lab users leave feedback or suggest improvements.
  • Lab-only equipment is hidden here for everyone. Lab managers and admins can turn on Show lab-only equipment.

Equipment QR labels

Scanning an equipment's QR label opens a page with its SOP, Book, Contact and Calibration options, after signing in. Options a lab user may not use for that item are hidden.

Section 06

📅 Booking Equipment

The shared booking calendar and its rules

Tabs: Book Equipment History & Usage Special Treatment Settings (admins)

Who can book what

  • TRAINING REQUIRED — greyed out; a lab user cannot select it until their training is approved.
  • RETRAIN — not used for 3 months; greyed out until the retraining is approved.
  • Bookings are in the booker's own name. Lab users cannot book for someone else. Lab managers can, with Book on behalf of in the booking form.

Making a booking

  1. Tick one or more instruments in the sidebar (Select all shows every booking on one calendar).
  2. With several ticked, choose Same time for all (one drag books them all) or One by one (a time for each).
  3. Drag down on the calendar to pick the day and time. Past times cannot be booked.
  4. Choose the purpose — Project, Thesis or Other — and tick "I am responsible for this booking and confirm it is for the purpose selected." Both are required.
  5. After booking, add it to Google Calendar, Outlook, or download an .ics file.

Approving, moving & cancelling

  • Equipment set to Requires Approval creates pending bookings. Click one, then Approve or Deny with a reason; the booker is notified.
  • Move a booking by dragging it; drag its top or bottom edge to change the time. A lab user can move only their own.
  • Cancel is possible until the booking starts. Lab users should cancel what they will not use, so others can book it.
  • Edit is possible until it ends; a booking in progress can be extended. A finished booking is locked — only admins can correct it.
  • View lab user (above the calendar) shows one person's bookings.
  • Some equipment asks for photos of the area before and after use; missing photos can block that person's future bookings.

History, special treatment & settings

  • History & Usage — past bookings, with CSV export.
  • Special Treatment — instructions shown to anyone booking that equipment, which they must confirm before booking.
  • Settings (admins) — which equipment can be booked, and which needs approval.
Section 07

📋 Task Board

Tasks, meetings, deadlines and reminders

Tabs: Overview My Tasks Team Calendar Timeline Meetings Reminders Assign Others (admins)

  • My Tasks — add a task with a deadline, priority and project; click its status circle to move it along. Moving a task to done sets it to 100%. Private tasks are hidden from teammates.
  • Out of Lab (My Tasks sidebar) — mark days you will be away; they show as a red ✕ on the calendars.
  • Team — every lab manager's tasks on one board.
  • Timeline — each task as a bar from start to deadline, grouped by project. Link tasks ("waits on") to see the critical path — the chain that decides the finish date. Tasks without dates are listed as Undated.
  • Meetings — schedule a meeting, then add action items for that date; the live drawing board lets you sketch together.
  • Reminders — tick Remind me daily on a task to get a reminder every morning at 7am until it is done (it does not start before the task's start date).
Section 08

📦 Supply Inventory

Room-by-room supply counts and reports

Tabs: Inspection Export Data Rooms Supplies Import Settings

  1. Inspection — pick a room and count each supply. The box starts at the last count (grey); leave it untouched to keep that number.
  2. A count below the minimum asks how many need ordering before you move on.
  3. On the results screen, Save & next room starts the next room that has supplies.
  • Export Data — one inspection or the full history, as Excel or PDF, with clickable purchase links. Edit corrects a count afterwards.
  • Rooms / Supplies — card grids with photos; supplies are grouped by room. Each supply has a unit and a minimum. Import loads a list from Excel.

💬 Lab Messages

Conversations kept as threads

  • Start a message, then reply inside its thread; attach a file or photo to a message or a reply.

  • Choose a background for the message board.
Section 10

🧪 {{PROJ}}

Projects, sub projects, material folders, reductions and labels

Sidebar: Material Inventory Tested Materials Manage Projects Material Types Project Test Results Workspace Project Members

The Projects · Materials · Search row switches between project cards, every material, and search. Lab users see the projects they are assigned to. Manage Projects creates projects and assigns lab users; Material Types sets the types offered when adding a material.

Sub projects

A project can be split into sub projects (optional), such as MIX or #2:

project → sub project → material folder → reductions

  • Open a project and its Sub projects tab. Add sub projects (up to 20 characters — the name is printed on the label), then tick the material folders that belong to each and click Save changes.
  • A folder can be in several sub projects; it then gets its own colour wherever it appears. Folders not ticked anywhere stay on the main project.
  • A project without sub projects shows a one-time "New: sub projects" message to everyone who can work on it.
  • A sub project can be removed only once no folder is connected to it.

Materials & folders

  • Open a project card; Add material to … appears. The first question is Sub project (required, main project last). The rest — name, PI, type, source, quantity, location, dates and photos — is as before.
  • Editing a material shows its sub projects as tick boxes.
  • The small sign beside 2 · Project Materials opens the folders page: project → sub projects → folders → reductions as a tree, with the project info in the left sidebar. Click a folder to see its details below.
  • Non-Project Material (Materials view) is for material that belongs to no project.

Material reduction

Material Reduction splits a material into new ones — for example fractionation into sieve sizes. Each fraction becomes its own material, with its own barcode, quantity, location and label, listed under its parent's Material Reduction tab (never as a separate card). Fractions follow their parent's sub projects.

Labels & tested samples

  • Every material has a printable 4×6 label with its own barcode and QR code. It lists Project info (with the sub project), Original Material, and — on a reduction — Reduced Material.
  • A tested sample gets its own label and identity: use the Tested pill (one label for samples from one or more projects), or the Tested materials label tab on a material. Tested Materials in the sidebar lists them all.
  • Project Test Results — record results and attach their files. Workspace — charts and analysis of the results. Project Members — team up so you share project data.

🔲 QR Labels

Print equipment and material labels

  • Equipment Barcode — make and print a QR label for an instrument. Labels print in black and white on any printer.

Section 12

👤 Profile & Account Settings

Your details, password, dashboard and notifications

Click your name at the top right, or the Profile card.

  • My Info — name, nickname, photo or avatar, and contact details.
  • Password — change your password (at least 6 characters).
  • Dashboard Icons — choose which cards appear on your dashboard. You can only choose from the modules your organization has given you.
  • Notifications — for each kind of event, choose in-app and/or email: bookings, training decisions, tasks and comments, meetings, deadline and daily reminders, messages, icons granted, and invitations.
  • Storage — where your personal files are saved: LabHive cloud, Google Drive, OneDrive, or your own server (WebDAV). Only future uploads move.
  • Delete Account — ask for your account to be removed; an admin reviews the request.
